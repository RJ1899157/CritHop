Corporate Travel Policy (Section 4.1 - Flight Class Eligibility): Full-time employees may book Business Class accommodations only for continuous international commercial flights exceeding eight (8) scheduled hours of flying time, provided that written Vice President approval is secured prior to booking.

Corporate Travel Policy (Section 4.3 - Executive Briefing Exception Clause): Notwithstanding Section 4.1, if an employee is scheduled to conduct or deliver a Tier-1 Client Executive Briefing within four (4) hours of scheduled airport arrival, the continuous flight duration requirement for Business Class eligibility is reduced from eight (8) hours to six (6) hours.

Corporate Travel Policy (Section 7.2 - Per Diem and Meal Allowances): Daily meal expenses are reimbursable up to a maximum per diem cap of $120.00 USD in designated Tier-1 metropolitan markets (London, Tokyo, New York City, Zurich, Singapore). Purchases of alcoholic beverages are strictly non-reimbursable unless incurred during an authorized Client Entertainment Dinner pre-approved under Section 8.4.
