Christie's London Auction Catalog (June 1924, Lot 44): Lot 44: 'Portrait of a Young Florentine Nobleman', oil and tempera on poplar panel, attributed to the Florentine workshop of Sandro Botticelli (circa 1485). Provenance recorded as the private collection of the late Duke of Sutherland. Purchased at public auction by Austrian textile industrialist Baron Viktor von Wertheim for 2,400 guineas, subsequently installed in the Wertheim family palace in Vienna.

Gestapo Property Seizure Inventory (Vienna, November 1938): Confiscation protocol recorded following the annexation of Austria (Anschluss), detailing the systematic seizure of private assets belonging to the Wertheim estate. Under order of the Reich Ministry of Finance, eighteen Renaissance and Baroque paintings were impounded and transferred to the Munich Central Collecting Point, designated under consolidated Reich inventory storage code `MK-482`.

Allied Monuments Men Field Report (Altaussee Salt Mine, May 1945): Sub-unit inspection report of Nazi art repositories discovered deep inside the Altaussee salt mine complex in Austria. Allied officers recovered repository crate `MK-482`. Due to damaged packing manifests and misidentified Cyrillic labeling during hasty postwar repatriation operations, artwork from crate `MK-482` was cataloged erroneously as originating from the aristocratic collection of Count Jan Potocki and transferred to the Polish Ministry of Culture in Warsaw in October 1948.

National Museum in Warsaw Accession Ledger (1951, Entry #W-1049): Accession Entry #W-1049 records the formal acquisition of an anonymous Florentine Renaissance portrait panel received from the postwar repatriation warehouse. Cleaned and attributed as 'Youth with Laurel' by museum curator Stanislaw Lorentz, the masterpiece has been displayed continuously in the Permanent Gallery of Italian Painting.

Restitution Claim Dossier (2023): Legal representatives of the surviving descendants of Baron Viktor von Wertheim submitted an archival restitution dossier to European provenance authorities, seeking to establish the historical custody trail and current institutional accession identity of the 1924 Christie's Botticelli workshop painting confiscated in Vienna in 1938.

Restoration Technical Notes (Distractor): Micro-spectroscopy of 17th-century Dutch landscape panels reveals natural dammar varnish oxidation, which can be safely solubilized using a 50/50 mixture of isopropanol and petroleum benzine without disturbing underlying glazes.

Biographical Workshop Sketch (Distractor): Sandro Botticelli completed his early formative apprenticeship under the master painter Fra Filippo Lippi in Prato before establishing his independent Florentine bottega around 1470.
